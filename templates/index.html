How to use these values

Azimuth — face the direction shown on the compass. For a wall mount, this is the horizontal bearing of the dish from behind.

Elevation — tilt the dish up from horizontal by this angle. Most motorised mounts show this on a protractor scale.

LNB Skew — rotate the LNB collar by this angle relative to its neutral position. Positive = clockwise, negative = anticlockwise, when viewed from behind the dish. UK/Astra 2 is typically +20–26° (clockwise). Some LNBs mark the angle on the collar; ±5° tolerance is fine.

How to use
1
Calculate the target pointing on the Alignment tab — enter your postcode and select Astra 2 (28.2°E).
2
Hold or rest the phone flat on the main dish arm (the long strut the LNB sits on), screen facing up, with the top of the phone pointing toward the satellite — roughly south-east for Astra 2 from the UK.
3
Tap Enable sensors. The crosshair dot shows where you are relative to the target. Move the dish until the dot reaches the centre and turns green.
4
When both azimuth and elevation are green, tighten the mount bolts, then rotate the LNB collar by the skew angle shown — clockwise for a positive value.
Crosshair scale: inner dashed ring = 5°, outer ring = 10°. Tolerance zones: azimuth ±2°, elevation ±1°.
Compass note: keep the phone away from the dish motor/clamp when reading azimuth — metal affects the magnetometer. Calibrate by waving the phone in a figure-8 if readings look wrong.
